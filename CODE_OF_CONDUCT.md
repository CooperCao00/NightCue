# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and code, and explain disagreements with evidence. Make room for people with different experience levels, languages, and backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock a conversation, or restrict participation when needed. They should explain moderation decisions when it is safe to do so. Use GitHub's reporting tools to report abuse or contact a maintainer through an available private channel; do not repost private details in a public issue.

请尊重不同经验、语言与背景的参与者，围绕观点和代码讨论，用事实说明分歧。不得骚扰、威胁、歧视、性骚扰或泄露他人隐私。

维护者可移除不当内容、关闭或锁定讨论，必要时限制参与；在安全可行时说明处理原因。遇到违规行为，请通过 GitHub 举报功能或维护者提供的私下渠道反馈，不要在公开 issue 中再次披露私人信息。
